United Voices for Animals

June 2026 3 min read Pamela Dormeus

United Voices for Animals, also known as UVFA, is a youth-led non-profit organization that works to raise awareness of animals in need and those less fortunate. We reach out to youth to encourage them to make an impact on animal welfare by volunteering at shelters and helping stray animals.

A tiny paw reaching for a big future — UVFA advocates for the love they deserve

UVFA was established to reach out to the youth nationwide regardless of where you are geographically in a way that can create a long-lasting effect for the future of animals through proper care, awareness, and advocacy. If you value and cherish animals as much as we do, our goal is to inspire animal lovers like you to go out into your community no matter where you are, and do something to make a change in any big or small way because even the littlest act can add to something much broader.

Compassion is an action — UVFA providing every stray with hope and love
Tales of Tails

Tales of tails

The Tales of Tails journal is a United Voices for Animals blog that will feature stories and entries that will highlight the issues that circulate around animal welfare, spotlight heartwarming stories about rescues, elaborate on topics for advocacy, awareness, and more. We will serve as your insight on anything that is animal-related to help you be a bit more aware and educated about what is going on regarding animal news and best circumstances and conditions for animal health.

A small action does more than you think — UVFA ensuring no stray gets left behind

Our future entries will further explore shelter support systems and sustainable pet ownership to ensure every animal gets the life conditions they rightfully deserve. Through these educational pieces written by our team, our goal is to provide you with the knowledge and information to better manage your pet's health and actively improve the lives of animals in need. If you are someone who shares our passion for animals we would love for you to stay tuned for the future plans of the Tales of Tails.
